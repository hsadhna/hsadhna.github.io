03

Risk managementHow I handle bad data

Processes to identify and manage risk

Every gap gets a written assumption

When a client couldn't tell me shipping ports or truck loads, I picked a defensible default, wrote it down, and gave them a list of data to collect next year.

No evidence, no credit

A company "committing" to report Scope 3 next year scores N. A framework index isn't proof. I record the page for every Y.

Follow the biggest number

In my SME inventory, Scope 3 was 98.8% of the total. That told the client to talk to suppliers before buying new boilers.

MetricsThe work

A carbon footprint and net-zero roadmap for a textile manufacturer

BMO SME CLIMATE CLINIC · UBC SAUDER · CLIMATE REPORTING ANALYST · TEAM OF 2

A Canadian knitting mill wanted its first carbon footprint and a plan it could afford to follow. Our first pass used dollars spent, which said little about where emissions came from.

I rebuilt the model around physical quantities: kilograms of yarn by fibre and country, freight in tonne-kilometres, commuting by mode. The result was several times higher than the spend-based number, and it showed exactly where the emissions sit.

Method
GHG Protocol, operational control, activity-based
Scope
Scopes 1 and 2, Scope 3 categories 1, 4, 6, 7 and 9
Finding
Purchased yarn and fabric are about 95% of Scope 3
Output
Four-phase roadmap to 2050 and a data request list
