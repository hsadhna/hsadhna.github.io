Carbon footprint and net-zero roadmap for a textile manufacturer

BMO SME CLIMATE CLINIC · CLIMATE REPORTING ANALYST · TEAM OF 2
01

A Canadian knitting mill wanted its first carbon footprint and a plan it could afford to follow. Our first pass used dollars spent, which said little about where emissions came from.

I rebuilt the model around physical quantities: kilograms of yarn by fibre and country, freight in tonne-kilometres, and commuting by mode. The total came out several times higher, and it showed exactly where the emissions sit.

What I did

  • Set the boundary under the GHG Protocol and documented every assumption
  • Split yarn emissions by fibre and sourcing country
  • Added employee commuting and business travel to Scope 3
  • Drafted a four-phase roadmap to 2050 and a data request list for the client

Benchmarking 100+ company climate disclosures against CSDS 2

CANADA CLIMATE LAW INITIATIVE · RESEARCH
02

I read sustainability and climate reports from TSX-listed companies and scored each one against the Canadian Sustainability Disclosure Standard 2, recording the page for every requirement met.

The hard part was consistency. The same edge cases came up again and again, and handling them the same way every time is what makes a benchmark worth using.
